# Washing Machine Troubleshooting

## Loud banging or knocking during the spin cycle

The most common cause is an unbalanced load. Pause the cycle, open the door and
redistribute the washing evenly around the drum. Bulky single items such as a
duvet or a bath mat are the usual culprits.

If the noise continues, check that all four levelling feet are in firm contact
with the floor and that the machine does not rock when pushed at a corner.

On a newly delivered machine, banging almost always means the **transit bolts
were not removed**. These are the bolts at the rear that hold the drum still in
transport. Damage caused by running a machine with transit bolts still fitted
is not covered by the warranty.

## Water leaking from the door

Check for clothing trapped in the rubber door seal, which stops the door
sealing. Wipe the seal and look for detergent residue, hair or grit built up in
the fold. Using more detergent than recommended causes excess foam, which can
push water past the seal.

## The machine will not drain

Check the drain filter, usually behind a small hatch at the bottom front. Place
a shallow tray underneath before opening it, as standing water will come out.
Also check that the drain hose is not kinked or pushed too far into the
standpipe.

## The drum will not turn

Confirm the door is fully latched, as the machine will not start otherwise. If
the drum turns freely by hand but not under power, the drive belt or motor may
have failed; this is covered under warranty.

## Error codes

Error codes beginning with E1 indicate a water supply problem: check that the
inlet tap is open and the inlet hose is not kinked. Codes beginning with E2
indicate a drainage problem; see the drain filter guidance above.
