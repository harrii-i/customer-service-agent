# Returns and Refunds

## Change-of-mind returns

Unused appliances may be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full
refund. The appliance must be in its original packaging with all accessories
and documentation. We arrange collection; there is no collection charge for
change-of-mind returns of large appliances.

## Faulty on arrival

An appliance that is faulty on arrival may be returned within 30 days for a
full refund or a free replacement, at the customer's choice. After 30 days, a
faulty appliance is handled under the warranty as a repair or replacement.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Appliances that have been installed and used, unless faulty.
- Made-to-order or custom-finish appliances.
- Consumables and spare parts once the packaging is opened.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the returned appliance
has been received and inspected. Inspection takes up to three working days, and
the refund reaches the account within a further five to ten working days
depending on the bank.

## Cancelling before delivery

An order may be cancelled free of charge at any point before it is dispatched.
Once dispatched, it is handled as a change-of-mind return.
