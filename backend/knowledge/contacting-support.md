# Contacting Support

## Hours

Phone and chat support is available Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm, and Saturday,
9am to 5pm. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## What to have ready

Having these to hand makes a first contact much faster:

- The order number, which is on the order confirmation email.
- The appliance model and serial number, from the rating plate inside the door
  or on the rear panel.
- Any error code shown on the display.
- A short description of when the problem happens — for example, only on a
  spin cycle, or only when the machine is full.

## Escalation

If an issue is not resolved on first contact, it is escalated to a senior
support engineer, who responds within one working day. A customer may ask for
escalation at any point.

## Engineer visits

Where an on-site visit is needed, appointments are offered as a morning or
afternoon slot. The appliance must be accessible, with space to pull it out
from its housing. Visits under warranty are free; visits for issues found not
to be covered are chargeable at the standard call-out rate quoted before the
visit is booked.
