# Warranty Policy

All major appliances purchased directly from us include a **two-year
manufacturer's warranty** from the date of delivery. Small appliances such as
kettles, toasters and handheld vacuums carry a **one-year warranty**.

## What is covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. This includes motors,
control boards, pumps, heating elements and factory-fitted seals. If a covered
part fails, we repair it at no charge, or replace the appliance if a repair is
not practical.

## What is not covered

- Damage caused by installation that did not follow the supplied instructions,
  including failure to remove transit bolts before first use.
- Cosmetic damage such as scratches, dents or discoloration.
- Consumable parts: filters, belts, light bulbs, hoses and detergent drawers.
- Damage from misuse, unauthorised repair, or use of the appliance in a
  commercial setting.
- Limescale or blockage damage in areas with hard water where no water softener
  or descaling routine was used.

## Extended cover

An optional extended warranty may be purchased within 30 days of delivery. It
extends parts and labour cover to five years from the delivery date.

## Making a claim

Claims require the order number and the appliance serial number, which is on
the rating plate inside the door or on the rear panel. A support agent raises
the claim and books an engineer visit; most visits are scheduled within five
working days.
