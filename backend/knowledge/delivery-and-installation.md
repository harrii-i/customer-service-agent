# Delivery and Installation

## Delivery

Standard delivery is within **three to seven working days**. A one-hour arrival
window is sent by text on the morning of delivery. Someone aged 18 or over must
be present to accept the delivery.

Large appliances are delivered to the room of choice on the ground floor as
standard. Delivery to an upper floor is available where stairs are safe and
clear.

## Installation

Installation may be added at checkout or booked later by contacting support. An
installation includes connecting the appliance, removing transit bolts, testing
a short cycle, and taking away the packaging.

Installation does not include plumbing or electrical work beyond connecting to
an existing, compliant supply. If a new supply point is required, a qualified
plumber or electrician must complete that work first.

## Removal of an old appliance

We can take away and recycle one old appliance of the same type on the day of
delivery. It must be disconnected and emptied before we arrive.

## Missed deliveries

If nobody is available, the driver leaves a card and the delivery is
rescheduled. Two consecutive missed deliveries may result in the order being
returned and refunded as a change-of-mind return.
